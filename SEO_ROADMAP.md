# Complete Free SEO Roadmap for Visual Idea

**Website:** [Visual Idea](https://visualidea.netlify.app/)  
**Niche:** Tools, resources, and viral ideas for content creators and the creator economy.

---

## Goal
Help Google and search engines understand:
- What the platform does (Creator productivity & growth).
- Who it helps (Content Creators, YouTubers, TikTokers, Influencers).
- What type of creators it serves (Digital creators, streamers, educators).
- Why your pages should rank for creator-focused keywords.

---

## PHASE 1 — Fix Basic SEO (Most Important)

### Step 1: Proper Title Tags
Every page needs a unique SEO title.
- **Homepage:** `Visual Idea | Content Creation Tools & Viral Ideas for Creators`
- **Library:** `Viral Content Ideas & Hook Library | Visual Idea`
- **Creator Tools:** `Best Content Creation Tools for Creators | Visual Idea`
- **AI Tools:** `Best AI Tools for Content Creators in 2026 | Visual Idea`

### Step 2: Meta Descriptions
`<meta name="description" content="Discover the best tools, AI resources, apps, and productivity solutions for content creators, YouTubers, influencers, and digital creators.">`

### Step 3: Heading Structure
Maintain hierarchy: `h1` -> `h2` -> `h3`.
- **H1:** `Tools & Resources for Content Creators`

### Step 4: SEO-Friendly Content
The homepage explains:
- What the platform does.
- Who it helps.
- What creators can discover.
- Why the platform is useful for audience growth.

### Step 5: SEO Landing Pages
Focused creator resource pages:
- `/content-ideas`
- `/ai-tools`
- `/workflow`
- `/growth`

---

## PHASE 2 — Technical SEO

### Step 6: Sitemap
Available at `/sitemap.xml`. Generated dynamically via `src/app/sitemap.ts`.

### Step 7: robots.txt
Available at `/robots.txt`. Points to the sitemap and guides crawlers.

### Step 8: Search Console
1. Add property.
2. Verify ownership (HTML file uploaded).
3. Submit sitemap.

### Step 9 & 10: Performance
- Mobile-responsive design for creators on the go.
- Image compression for fast loading of tool screenshots.
- Lazy loading enabled.
